    About Our Platform

    This static directory brings together selected activity listings and official public-nature catalogues. It is not affiliated with the providers and does not guarantee that an offer, rate or schedule is current.

    Official Sources

    Listings were checked against linked provider or public-agency pages where accessible. Some providers’ sites were unavailable or did not publish an activity catalogue, so those offers are not represented as confirmed.

    Catalogue Scope

    Commercial offers can be seasonal or customized, while public nature inventories can be very large. The directory is a selection, not a complete list of every activity in Estonia.
