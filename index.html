    🌿 The Story Behind Estonia Activities

    Why I Built This Guide

    When I first visited Estonia, before eventually moving here, I wanted to do more than just walk around sightseeing. I wanted to get out into the bogs, try local crafts, paddle through flooded forests, and actually experience the country.

    I quickly realized how hard it was to find practical advice in one place. Planning a single weekend meant piecing together clues across dozens of scattered websites, many of which barely explained what the activity actually involved, what it cost, or when to go.

    I created Estonia Activities to solve that problem. Every listing here is hand-picked and researched so you can see exactly what you'll do, how long it takes, what's included, and which season works best, with direct links to the local guides and trails that make Estonia unforgettable.
