Field & Expedition Guidelines

Self-Guided Trails & Gear

Free RMK boardwalks and forest trails require travelers to bring all specialized clothing, boots, and water[cite: 1]. Campfire wood is provided free at designated state forest shelters[cite: 1].

Official Permits & Licensing

Recreational fishing on Lake Peipus and Baltic waters legally requires an online state license via kalaluba.ee[cite: 1]. Drone usage over national parks requires prior Environmental Board clearance.

Expedition Reservations

Early direct reservations (at least 2–4 weeks in advance) are necessary for brown bear hides, winter husky sled teams, and Malusi seal safaris due to strict ecological quota limits[cite: 1].
